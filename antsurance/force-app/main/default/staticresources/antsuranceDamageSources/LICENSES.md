# Sample damage photographs

Nine photographs used to demonstrate and test the photo assessment on claims (the `antsuranceDamage*` static resources).
All come from Wikimedia Commons and are public domain or under a Creative Commons license that allows reuse and changes with credit.
Each was resized to at most 1,280 pixels on its long edge; crops are noted. None shows a person, a legible number plate or, on the rear quarter and deer strike photographs, a maker's badge.
